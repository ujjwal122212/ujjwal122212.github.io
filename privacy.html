Privacy Policy

Lakshvi — personal finance app for Android · Last updated: 1 October 2026

Lakshvi helps you track accounts, transactions, budgets and savings plans. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have. In short: your financial data stays on your phone and in your own Google Drive. We do not run any server, and we never see, sell or share your data.

1. Information the app uses

  • Google account details — when you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address and profile photo. These are used only to show your profile inside the app and to connect your backup to your account.
  • Financial information you enter — accounts, balances, transactions, categories, budget plans, cycles and events that you type into the app.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID, and it contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.

2. Where your data is stored

  • On your device. Your financial data is stored in a database on your phone, encrypted with a key protected by Android's secure hardware (Keystore).
  • In your own Google Drive (backup). If you are signed in, the app backs up an encrypted copy of your database about once a day to the hidden "app data" folder of your Google Drive. This folder is private to the Lakshvi app and your Google account: it is not visible in your Drive file list and no one else, including us, can access it. The key used to encrypt the backup is stored in the same private folder, so signing in with the same Google account on another phone can restore your data.

We do not operate any server and do not receive a copy of your data.

3. Google user data

Lakshvi requests these Google permissions:

  • email and profile — to identify your account and show your name and photo.
  • drive.appdata — to create, read and update the app's own backup files in the hidden app data folder of your Google Drive. The app cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

Lakshvi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the sign-in and backup features described here. It is never transferred to anyone else, never used for advertising, and never read by humans.

4. App lock and biometrics

If you turn on App Lock, unlocking is handled entirely by Android's built-in fingerprint, face or screen-lock system. The app only receives a yes/no result — it never has access to your fingerprint, face data or PIN.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial information with any third party. Data only leaves your phone when the app backs it up to your own Google Drive, using Google's encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

6. Deleting your data

  • On the phone: open Settings → Danger Zone → Delete All Data, or uninstall the app.
  • Your Drive backup: on the web, open Google Drive → Settings (gear icon) → Manage apps → Lakshvi → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Google access: you can remove Lakshvi's access to your Google account at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Security

Your data is encrypted on the device and in your Drive backup, and you can add App Lock for extra protection. No method of storage is 100% secure, so please also keep your phone and Google account protected with a strong screen lock and password.

8. Children

Lakshvi is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
